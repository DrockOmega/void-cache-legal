Privacy Policy

For Void Cache: Space Scavenger · Last updated: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Void Cache: Space Scavenger ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is a single-player idle game. It has no user accounts, no login, and does not ask you for your name, email, or any other personal contact information. By installing and using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information the App handles

We do not collect personal information directly. Data handling falls into three categories:

  • Game progress (stored only on your device). Your save data — resources, upgrades, timers, and settings — is stored locally on your device using standard Android on-device storage. It is not transmitted to us and we cannot read it.
  • Advertising data (via Google AdMob). The App shows opt-in rewarded video advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure these ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID), along with device information, coarse (IP-derived) location, and ad-interaction data. This processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Purchase data (via Google Play Billing). If you buy an optional in-app product (the "Salvager's License"), the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card or billing details — we receive only whether the purchase succeeded, so the App can unlock the purchased feature.

2. How this information is used

  • To save and restore your game progress on your device.
  • To display opt-in rewarded advertisements and grant the associated in-game bonus.
  • To deliver and restore in-app purchases you choose to make.
  • To send local reminder notifications (e.g. when a timer completes). These are generated on your device and are not sent to us.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles ourselves.

3. Third-party services

The App relies on the following third parties, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — Google Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google Play Billing / Google Play Services (purchases and app distribution) — Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices and controls

  • Advertising ID. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a bonus. Declining costs you nothing in the App.
  • Clearing data. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage removes your local save data from your device.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, please contact us so we can address it.

6. Data retention and security

Game progress remains on your device until you delete it. Data processed by Google (ads, purchases) is retained and secured according to Google's policies. Data exchanged with Google's services is encrypted in transit.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
